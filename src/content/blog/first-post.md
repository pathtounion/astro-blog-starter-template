An ontological primitive is a basic irreducible entity. Materialists 
believe that matter is fundamental. Consciousness is viewed as an emergent property of matter
which has led to the hard problem of consciousness. Explaining how consciousness emerges from
matter is "hard". Analytic idealists believe consciousness is the ontological primitive of
reality.  Matter is an excitation of consciousness, so analytic idealists are not faced with a
hard problem explaining how matter emerges from consciousness.
